The app is in Polish. No login is required. A/a, Ą/ą, B/b, C/c, Ć/ć, D/d and E/e are free. Tap any locked tile to open the full-version dialog. A single non-consumable product (full_version) unlocks all letters and digits. Purchasing and restoring require the parent gate (enter the adult’s four-digit birth year; checked locally, never stored or transmitted). Restore is also available under Settings → Odblokuj wszystkie literki. There are no ads, social features, user accounts, or behavioral analytics. Writing and progress stay on the device. RevenueCat verifies transactions. The initial in-app purchase must be attached to this first version in App Store Connect. No web checkout or external payment links are used.
